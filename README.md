# MorphMan (JoMako)

Find Japanese media at your level and learn the words you need for it, right inside Anki.

MorphMan reads the Japanese in your Anki cards and works out which words you know. With that, you can:

- **Find media at your level**: anime, video games, movies, TV, manga, visual novels…
- **See which words you're missing** to reach your goal
- **Learn those words** with your own cards, or with new cards made from real example sentences
- **Reorder your new cards** so the ones with only one unknown word (k+1) come first

## Install

In Anki: **Tools > Add-ons > Get Add-ons**, and enter the code **`73983788`** ([AnkiWeb page](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/73983788)).

You can also download the `.ankiaddon` file of the [latest release](https://github.com/Jo-Mako-Anki/morphman/releases/latest) and double-click it. It is the same version as on AnkiWeb, and AnkiWeb updates will replace it.

On first start, MorphMan offers to download the Japanese dictionary (MecabUnidic, about 500 MB). Restart Anki once it's done.

If you have an older MorphMan installed, disable it in Tools > Add-ons first.
