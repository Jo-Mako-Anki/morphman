# Readability

**Tools > MorphMan > Readability** (Ctrl+R).

This tab shows how much of a media you can read with the words you already know.

It has 2 parts:

1. [Compare Media](compare-media.md): pick what to analyze, then **Analyze!**
2. [Study Plan](study-plan.md): what you still have to learn, and in which order.

The results are in the tables under them. See [Table Data](../tables/index.md).
