# Episodes

The results of a season, one row per episode.

Right-click a row of a show in [Results](results.md), then **Results by episode**. Its files are worked out in this tab, one row each.

It has the same columns as Results. You can mark an episode as Done here too.

Double-click an episode to see its words to learn, as on a row of Results.

Handy to know which episode to watch first, or to follow a show episode by episode.
