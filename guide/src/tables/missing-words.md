# Missing Words

The words of the Master Frequency List you don't know yet, in the order of the list. Click **Generate report** to make it.

No analysis needed: without one, the examples come from the list itself.

It has the same columns as the [Results Frequency List](frequency-list.md). Spread comes from the master list.

The words you know are hidden. Right-click the **Known** header to show them.

No Master Frequency List? Choose one in the [Study Plan](../readability/study-plan.md#master-frequency-list).

**Save as file…** saves the list as a text file.
