# Results Frequency List

Every word of the titles you analyzed, the most frequent first. Click **Generate report** to make it.

The rows of the words you don't know are tinted.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Count** | How many times the word appears. |
| **Norm**, **Base**, **Reading** | The word: its dictionary form, its form in the text, its reading in hiragana. |
| **POS**, **Sub-POS** | The type of word, with the names of Settings > Part of Speech Names. |
| **Rank** | Its place in the list: 1 is the most frequent. |
| **Percent** | Its share of all the words. |
| **Cumulative_Percent** | The share of all the words down to this row. |
| **Known** | Yes or No. |
| **Example** | A sentence with this word. |
| **Spread** | The share of the titles that have the word: how widely it's spread. |

Right-click POS or Sub-POS to filter. Right-click Norm, Base or Reading to search. Right-click a number column to filter by range: `100-500`, `<500`, `>=1000`.

The list is shown 100 rows at a time. Use the arrows at the bottom to change page.

## Save as file

**Save as file…** saves the list as a text file.

That file can be used as a **Master Frequency List**, in the [Study Plan](../readability/study-plan.md#master-frequency-list).
