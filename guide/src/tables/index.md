# Table Data

After **Analyze!**, the results are in tabs, under Compare Media and Study Plan:

1. [Results](results.md): one row per title.
2. [Episodes](episodes.md): one row per episode of a season.
3. [Results Frequency List](frequency-list.md): every word of the analyzed titles.
4. [Missing Words](missing-words.md): the words of the master list you don't know yet.
5. [learning_order.txt](learning-order.md): the file the Study Plan wrote.

In every table:

- Click a header to sort.
- Right-click a header to filter or search.
- Double-click a word to see its k+1 cards in the Browser. Shift + double-click: every new card with this word.
