# Words to learn

Select a title in [Results](../tables/results.md), then click **Words to learn…** (or double-click the row).

You get the unknown words of the title, the most frequent first.

The window has 3 parts:

1. [Settings](settings.md): which words are shown.
2. [Words](words.md): the table of words.
3. [Create](create.md): select words, then make cards or files with them.

And 2 tabs: **Words** and **Coverage**.
