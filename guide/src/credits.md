## Dictionaries and tools

- **[MeCab](https://taku910.github.io/mecab/)**, by Taku Kudo: cuts Japanese into words.
- **[UniDic](https://clrd.ninjal.ac.jp/unidic/)**, by NINJAL (National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics): the dictionary MeCab uses.
- **[MecabUnidic](https://github.com/ianki/MecabUnidic)**, by ianki: MeCab and UniDic 3.1.0 as an Anki add-on.
- **[Jitendex](https://jitendex.org)**, by Stephen Kraus, under CC BY-SA 4.0: the meanings in the Definition field and Words to learn. It includes JMdict, from the [Electronic Dictionaries Research Group](https://www.edrdg.org/), and example sentences from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/) (CC BY 2.0 FR).

Thanks to everyone who tested and gave feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/J9Sw8jdkPY).
