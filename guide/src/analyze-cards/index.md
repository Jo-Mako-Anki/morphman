# Analyze Cards

**Tools > MorphMan > Analyze Cards.**

This is where you tell MorphMan which cards to read, and what to do with them.

The tab has 3 sections:

1. [Identify Cards](identify-cards.md): which cards MorphMan reads.
2. [Modify Cards](modify-cards.md): the fields and tags MorphMan writes in them.
3. [Recalc](recalc.md): counts your words, writes, and sorts your cards.
