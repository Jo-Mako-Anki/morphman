# More

Everything is in **Tools > MorphMan**:

| Menu | What it does | Shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| **Settings** | All the options. See [Settings](../setup.md). | Ctrl+O |
| **Analyze Cards** | Which cards MorphMan reads, and what it writes in them. | Ctrl+A |
| **Readability** | How much of a media you can read, and the words you're missing. | Ctrl+R |
| **Database Manager** | Compare and merge word databases. | Ctrl+D |
| **Recalc** | Counts your words and sorts your cards. | Ctrl+M |
| **Full Recalc** | The same, over every note. | Ctrl+Shift+M |

The usual way:

1. [Analyze Cards](../analyze-cards/index.md): tell MorphMan which cards to read.
2. [Recalc](../analyze-cards/recalc.md): count your words and sort your new cards.
3. [Readability](../readability/index.md): find media at your level.
4. [Add to deck](../words/add-cards.md): turn the words you're missing into cards.
5. Study. Recalc again from time to time.

## While you study

Two keys in the reviewer:

- **K**: the card is already known. MorphMan tags it `mm_AK` and skips to the next one.
- **L**: opens the Browser on the cards with the same focus morph.

The skip options (comprehension cards, fresh vocabulary…) are in [Settings > Review](../setup.md#review).
